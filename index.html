How this platform is organised

The Tutor and Speaking Room understand Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, and English, so you can learn Arabic or Urdu through whichever language feels most natural. The Languages section gives each of Arabic, Urdu, and English its own space with practice sentences, short stories, and book suggestions.

Tutor Chat

Ask about Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, or English — grammar, vocabulary, script, or translation. Vaani explains across all four.
